Reverend Dr. Peterson Mwangi

Senior Pastor | PEFA Ruiru East Community Church

Reverend Dr. Peterson Mwangi is a visionary spiritual leader with over 20 years of ministerial experience. He holds a Doctorate in Theology and has dedicated his life to spreading the Gospel with passion, integrity, and deep scriptural insight.

He is married to Pastor Carol Mwangi, and together they have been blessed with three children.
